# 10 Short Training Video Scripts

Short-form scripts (15–45 sec each) mapped to the 4-week beginner plan. Each has a hook, a demo, a coaching cue, and a call-to-action.

> This content is for general fitness and longevity education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have an existing health condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

---

**1. The beginner squat**
- Hook: "Most people squat wrong on rep one. Here's the fix."
- Demo: bodyweight squat, side angle.
- Cue: "Sit back like there's a chair behind you. Chest tall."
- CTA: "Save this for leg day."

**2. Incline push-ups for absolute beginners**
- Hook: "Can't do a push-up yet? Start here."
- Demo: hands on a bench, slow lower.
- Cue: "Straight line from head to heels."
- CTA: "Follow for the full beginner plan."

**3. Dumbbell row, no gym needed**
- Hook: "One move for a stronger back at home."
- Demo: single-arm row.
- Cue: "Drive the elbow back, squeeze, control the way down."
- CTA: "Comment 'PLAN' for the 4-week guide."

**4. The glute bridge**
- Hook: "The most underrated beginner exercise."
- Demo: glute bridge.
- Cue: "Push through your heels, squeeze at the top."
- CTA: "Try 3 sets of 12 tonight."

**5. How to hold a plank**
- Hook: "Stop letting your hips sag."
- Demo: plank, side view.
- Cue: "Brace like someone's about to poke your stomach."
- CTA: "Build to 40 seconds by week 3."

**6. Why beginners should walk**
- Hook: "The fat-loss tool everyone ignores."
- Demo: brisk walking clip.
- Cue: "10 minutes after every session. Easy and repeatable."
- CTA: "Add it to your plan today."

**7. Progress without adding weight**
- Hook: "You don't need heavier dumbbells yet."
- Demo: same move, more reps.
- Cue: "Add a rep or two before you add load."
- CTA: "Follow for smart progression."

**8. Warm-up in 60 seconds**
- Hook: "Skip this and your session suffers."
- Demo: march, arm circles, slow squats.
- Cue: "Five minutes, every time."
- CTA: "Save this warm-up."

**9. Build a balanced plate**
- Hook: "Eat for energy without counting every calorie."
- Demo: plate with protein, veg, carbs, fats.
- Cue: "Palm of protein, half a plate of veg."
- CTA: "Grab the free healthy-eating guide."

**10. Consistency beats intensity**
- Hook: "Three steady weeks beat one brutal one."
- Demo: training montage.
- Cue: "Show up, keep it sustainable, recover well."
- CTA: "Start the 4-week beginner plan."
