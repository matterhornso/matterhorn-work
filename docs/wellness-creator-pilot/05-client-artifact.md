# Your 4-Week Beginner Program

Welcome! This is your simple, do-anywhere starting program: three short sessions a week, a general healthy-eating guide, and everything you need to follow along on your own.

> This content is for general fitness and longevity education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have an existing health condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

## What's inside

1. **Your training plan** — a 4-week, 3-day-per-week full-body plan that builds gradually.
2. **Your session handouts** — one card per workout, so you always know what to do.
3. **Your healthy-eating guide** — five everyday habits, a grocery list, and meal-prep ideas.

## The 4 weeks at a glance

- **Week 1 — Learn the moves.** Light, clean form. Build the habit.
- **Week 2 — Add a little volume.** A couple more reps, a slightly longer walk.
- **Week 3 — Add a little load.** Slightly heavier where form is solid.
- **Week 4 — Consolidate.** Lock it in, then reassess how far you've come.

## Your weekly checklist

- [ ] 3 training sessions done
- [ ] Warm-up + cool-down each time
- [ ] A balanced plate most meals
- [ ] Water through the day
- [ ] Rest when you feel run down

## How to get help

If anything hurts, feels off, or you have a health condition, stop and check in with a qualified healthcare professional. Your coach can adjust the plan — message them any time.
