# General Healthy-Eating Guide

A simple, general guide to pair with the 4-week beginner plan. It is about building sustainable habits — not a strict or clinical diet.

> This content is for general fitness and longevity education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have an existing health condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

> This guidance is general healthy-eating information, not a clinical or therapeutic diet. It is not a substitute for care from a registered dietitian or doctor.

## Five everyday habits

1. **Build each plate around protein and vegetables.** A palm of protein, half a plate of vegetables, a fist of carbs, a thumb of fats.
2. **Drink water through the day.** Keep a bottle visible; have a glass with each meal.
3. **Prep one thing ahead.** Cook extra protein or chop vegetables once, use them twice.
4. **Slow down at meals.** Eat without screens; give it 15–20 minutes.
5. **Keep treats in proportion, not off-limits.** Restriction tends to backfire; balance lasts.

## Example grocery list

- **Protein:** eggs, chicken, white fish, Greek yoghurt, lentils, tofu.
- **Vegetables & fruit:** spinach, peppers, broccoli, tomatoes, bananas, berries, apples.
- **Carbs:** oats, brown rice, potatoes, wholegrain bread.
- **Fats:** olive oil, nuts, avocado.
- **Easy extras:** tinned beans, frozen vegetables, herbs and spices.

## Simple meal-prep ideas

- **Breakfast:** overnight oats with yoghurt and berries.
- **Lunch:** grain bowl — rice, chicken or tofu, mixed vegetables, olive oil.
- **Dinner:** baked fish or beans with potatoes and a big salad.
- **Snacks:** fruit, a handful of nuts, yoghurt.

## A note on goals

General healthy-eating habits support most people's energy and training. Individual needs differ. For anything specific — a medical condition, pregnancy, allergies, or a therapeutic diet — work with a registered dietitian or doctor.
