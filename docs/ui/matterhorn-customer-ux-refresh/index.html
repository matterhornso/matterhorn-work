Non-Custodial Safety Architecture
Matterhorn Work is designed so that your keys, your funds, and your signing capability always stay in your wallet.

No. Matterhorn Work is a non-custodial application. It never asks for, stores, or handles your private keys, seed phrases, or API secrets. All signing happens in your own wallet, outside of Matterhorn.
Matterhorn sees only the public order terms you specify (asset, side, size, price) and the public receipt returned from the venue (order ID or tx hash). It never receives your wallet signature.
No. Every order must be signed and submitted by you using your own wallet and the venue's official client or SDK. Matterhorn generates the order terms and a signing template, but never executes the transaction itself.
Polymarket previews are blocked for restricted jurisdictions. Matterhorn shows an amber compliance-blocked state instead of a preview. Hyperliquid markets are available on Base and Base Sepolia, which are permissionless — no jurisdiction restrictions apply.
The external signer model means Matterhorn prepares order terms but you, the user, are the sole signer. Your wallet computes the cryptographic signature. Matterhorn has no access to it. This is enforced technically: canSubmit is always false, and Matterhorn never accepts raw signatures in receipt imports.
Design notes: Safety explainer screen with visual architecture diagram (SVG flow showing Matterhorn → Public Terms → Your Wallet → Receipt Import). FAQ accordion with 5 questions covering keys, data visibility, submission, jurisdiction, and the external signer model. NO custody messaging ("your funds are safe with Matterhorn"). Language is precise: "you are the sole signer", "Matterhorn never holds keys".
