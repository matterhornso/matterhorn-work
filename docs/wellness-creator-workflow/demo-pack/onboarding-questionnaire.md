# New Client Onboarding Questionnaire

*Customer Demo Pack artifact — a reusable onboarding questionnaire for a personal trainer, yoga instructor, or dietician. Collects general, non-clinical context only.*

> This content is for general fitness and wellness education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have an existing health condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

## About you

1. What's your main goal for the next few weeks? (e.g. general fitness, mobility, healthy-eating habits)
2. What's your experience level with this kind of program?
3. How many days per week can you realistically commit?
4. What equipment or space do you have available?
5. What time of day works best for you?

## General context *(non-clinical)*

6. Is there anything that affects how you move or eat that your coach should keep in mind day to day? *(General preferences only — for any medical condition, injury, pregnancy, or specific dietary needs, please consult a qualified healthcare professional. Those are out of scope here.)*
7. What's worked, or not worked, for you before?

## Consent

8. I understand this program is **general fitness and wellness education, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment**, and that I should consult a qualified healthcare professional about any medical concern.  ☐

## Note for the creator

If a client raises a medical condition, injury, pregnancy, or an eating concern, **refer them to a qualified healthcare professional** rather than adjusting the program to work around it.

## Disclaimer

Results vary between individuals. No specific outcome or result is guaranteed. This questionnaire collects general, non-clinical information only.
