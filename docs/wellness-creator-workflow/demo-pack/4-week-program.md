# 4-Week Program

*Customer Demo Pack artifact — a reusable 4-week program a personal trainer, yoga instructor, or dietician can adapt. Educational/general longevity only.*

> This content is for general fitness and longevity education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have an existing health condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

## How to use

- Follow the weekly structure; progress gradually (add reps or minutes before adding load or intensity).
- Warm up and cool down each session. Stop anything that causes sharp pain and check in with a qualified professional.

## Week-by-week structure

### Training (personal trainer)
- **Week 1 — Learn the movements:** 3 full-body sessions, light, clean form.
- **Week 2 — Add volume:** a couple more reps per movement; longer brisk walk.
- **Week 3 — Add a little load:** slightly heavier where form is solid.
- **Week 4 — Consolidate:** repeat week 3, then reassess.

### Yoga / mobility (yoga instructor)
- **Week 1:** gentle mobility flow + breathing.
- **Week 2:** add range and hold times.
- **Week 3:** add a balance/strength element.
- **Week 4:** flowing sequence + relaxation.

### Healthy-eating (dietician)
- **Week 1:** build balanced plates; add a vegetable to two meals a day.
- **Week 2:** prep one thing ahead; consistent meal times.
- **Week 3:** hydration and mindful eating.
- **Week 4:** consolidate the habits that worked.

## Progression notes

"Progress" means small, steady increases — not jumping ahead. A week that feels too hard is a signal to repeat it, not to push through pain.
