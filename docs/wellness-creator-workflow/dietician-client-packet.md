## Offer: "Healthy-Eating Reset (General)"

**Who it's for:** clients who want sustainable, general healthy-eating habits — not a clinical or therapeutic diet.

**What's included**
- A client intake questionnaire (goals, preferences, schedule, non-clinical context).
- A general meal-planning template (balanced plates, grocery list, prep ideas).
- A weekly check-in to track habits the client self-reports.

## Important boundaries

- General healthy-eating habits only. This packet does **not** diagnose, and it is **not** a treatment for any condition.
- Anything specific — a medical condition, pregnancy, allergies, an eating disorder, or a therapeutic diet — is referred to a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.
