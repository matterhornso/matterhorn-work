# Dietician — Full Client Lifecycle

*A reproducible, customer-demo-safe walk-through of the complete client-delivery path for a dietician. General healthy-eating education only; artifact-first and offline: nothing is hosted, charged, emailed, or gated.*

> This content is for general fitness and wellness education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any nutrition program, especially if you have an existing health condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

> This guidance is general healthy-eating information, not a clinical or therapeutic diet. It is not a substitute for care from a registered dietitian or doctor.

## 1. Lead intake
Capture a prospective client's general goals, food preferences, schedule, and general non-clinical context. No medical condition is assessed or diagnosed.

## 2. Service offer
"Healthy-Eating Reset (General)" — what's included, plus **placeholder pricing only**. No payment is processed; payments are a planned, not-live Matterhorn service hook.

## 3. Onboarding questionnaire
Confirm preferences, routine, and consent. Anything clinical — a medical condition, pregnancy, allergies, or an eating disorder — is referred to a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

## 4. Weekly program
A general meal-planning template: balanced plates, a grocery list, and simple prep ideas. General healthy-eating information, not a clinical or therapeutic diet.

## 5. Progress check-in
A weekly check-in on habits the client self-reports (e.g. meals prepped, water, energy). No clinical metrics are diagnosed.

## 6. Renewal / follow-up
A follow-up note with a habit recap and next-step options. Pricing is a draft only; no payment is processed.

## 7. Client handoff packet
A wrap-up packet: a summary of the habits covered, a self-reported recap, and general next steps the client can keep or share.

## Disclaimer

Results vary between individuals. No specific outcome or result is guaranteed. This lifecycle is general nutrition education, not medical care. Future Matterhorn hooks — storage/hosting, payments, email, identity/access — are planned, not live.
