## Offer: "4-Week Mobility & Calm Program"

**Who it's for:** general students who want a gentle mobility and relaxation practice — including desk workers with tight hips and shoulders.

**What's included**
- A 4-week class program (one flow per week, with cueing notes).
- A client intake questionnaire (experience, schedule, general non-clinical context).
- One short video script for a featured sequence.
- A weekly check-in for adherence and how the practice feels.

## Important boundaries

- General mobility and relaxation education only. This program is **not** a treatment for back pain, injury, or any condition.
- Pregnancy-specific, injury-specific, or medical needs are referred to a qualified healthcare professional.
