## 4. Vision And Core Narrative

### The Problem

Chat is useful for answers, but serious work requires more:

- context that survives the conversation;
- tools that understand the job at hand;
- clear boundaries before consequential actions;
- outputs that can be reviewed and reused;
- evidence of what happened, what was approved, and what remains unfinished.

General chat interfaces often flatten every task into the same blank box. Specialist tools often assume too much expertise and scatter context across tabs, wallets, files, and dashboards.

### The Matterhorn Idea

Matterhorn Desks organizes AI work around focused desks. A desk combines:

- a domain-specific starting point;
- natural-language conversation;
- approved tools and data sources;
- structured previews and workflows;
- explicit review boundaries;
- project-owned notes, memory, outputs, and evidence.

### The Customer Promise

> Start with a conversation. End with work you can understand, review, and keep.

### What Makes Matterhorn Desks Different

1. **Focused desks, not one generic assistant.** Each desk starts with the context, tools, and workflow of a real job.
2. **Actions stay reviewable.** Matterhorn prepares consequential actions and shows where review, signing, or submission happens.
3. **Memory is visible.** Nothing becomes durable memory without an explicit review path.
4. **Evidence stays attached.** Outputs, receipts, notes, and decisions remain connected to the work that produced them.
5. **One workspace across app and agent tools.** Matterhorn MCPs can bring approved capabilities into supported coding-agent clients.

## 5. Who Matterhorn Desks Is For

### Primary Audiences

#### Protocol Operators And Researchers

People working with networks such as Bittensor who need to understand public wallet state, compare subnets or validators, prepare actions, and preserve evidence without handing custody to an AI system.

**Job to be done:** “Help me understand the protocol, evaluate my options, and prepare the next step safely.”

#### Market Researchers And Active Traders

People who want market context, orderbook and funding data, watchlists, compliance checks, and precise order previews before using their own wallet or client.

**Job to be done:** “Turn market information into a reviewed decision without hiding risk or acting behind my back.”

#### Longevity Creators And Coaches

Coaches, trainers, yoga instructors, and other non-clinical service professionals who need structured intake, program planning, client-ready artifacts, service packaging, and reusable workflow context.

**Job to be done:** “Turn my expertise and client constraints into a consistent, professional workflow.”

#### AI Agent Operators And Technical Teams

People using Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, or compatible MCP clients who want Matterhorn workspace, protocol, memory, workflow, and evidence tools available in their existing environment.

**Job to be done:** “Give my agent approved access to Matterhorn tools while keeping permissions and serious actions visible.”

### Not The Primary Fit

Matterhorn Desks is not designed for users seeking:

- custody of funds or keys;
- invisible or autonomous trading;
- guaranteed investment outcomes;
- medical diagnosis or treatment;
- a generic social chatbot;
- an unreviewed agent with unrestricted access to a computer or wallet.

## 6. The Product Model

### Core Concepts

| Concept      | Meaning                                                                             |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project      | The workspace that contains chats, notes, memory, outputs, and evidence.            |
| Desk         | A focused AI surface for a protocol or professional workflow.                       |
| Conversation | The natural-language interface used to ask, refine, and decide.                     |
| Tool         | An approved capability used to read data, prepare a preview, or create an artifact. |
| Review       | The point where the user verifies exact terms before a serious action continues.    |
| Handoff      | A prepared action completed in the user's wallet, signer, or external client.       |
| Evidence     | Receipts, outputs, notes, decisions, and history attached to the project.           |

### Primary User Loop

1. Open a project.
2. Choose a desk or start a general conversation.
3. Ask in normal language.
4. Review the response, sources, preview, or artifact.
5. Approve, edit, save, or complete the action in the appropriate external surface.
6. Keep the resulting note, output, receipt, or decision with the project.

### Important Interaction Rule

Opening a desk or choosing a starter task never silently sends a prompt or executes an action. The user can inspect and edit what happens next.

## 9. Chat And AI Experience

### Publishable Copy

> **A conversation with working context.**
>
> Matterhorn Desks keeps the project, desk, tools, permissions, and prior work in view so each useful answer can become a next step.

### Product Truth

Normal chat runs through the Matterhorn Desks engine as a managed agent session. It is not a stateless, one-shot call to a language model. The session can include:

- project and session context;
- tool permissions;
- bounded tool calls;
- workspace files;
- approval state;
- context compaction;
- persistent session history;
- provider and model selection when supported by the deployment.

### Internal Guardrail

Do not expose internal runtime names as the product. Public copy should say **Matterhorn Desks engine**. Compatibility names may still appear in package names, local paths, command-line examples, or technical documentation.

### Response Perspectives

Users can choose how a response is framed:

| Perspective | Public explanation                                                      |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cautious    | Leads with material risks, reversibility, and failure cases.            |
| Balanced    | Gives a direct answer with evidence and tradeoffs. This is the default. |
| Optimistic  | Leads with realistic opportunities while retaining material caveats.    |

Matterhorn produces one answer in the selected perspective. Perspective changes presentation only. It never weakens wallet review, financial risk, compliance, medical boundaries, permissions, or approval gates.

#### Suggested Website Copy

> **Choose the lens, not the safety rules.**
>
> Frame a response as Cautious, Balanced, or Optimistic while the underlying tools and safeguards stay the same.

## 11. Wallets, Review, And Safety

### Core Position

Matterhorn Desks is non-custodial. It does not hold seed phrases or private keys, silently sign, or move funds on the user's behalf.

### Approved Safety Copy

> **You approve what happens next.**
>
> Matterhorn can read public data, prepare a reviewable action, and show the exact handoff. Signing stays with you and the wallet or signer you choose.

### Serious-Action Flow

1. **Request:** the user asks for a read, comparison, preview, or action.
2. **Preview:** Matterhorn shows terms, sources, risk, freshness, and submit state.
3. **Policy and simulation:** supported flows check configured limits and expected consequences.
4. **Review:** the user verifies exact terms.
5. **Approval or handoff:** the user's connected wallet, external signer, or official client completes the action.
6. **Receipt:** public result evidence can be attached to the project.

### Protocol Action Matrix

| Surface                        | Matterhorn can do                                                                         | Matterhorn does not do                        |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Bittensor                      | Read, compare, preview, watch, prepare handoff, verify public receipt                     | Hold keys, sign, submit, or broadcast         |
| Hyperliquid chat/MCP/CLI/watch | Read, analyze, watch, prepare preview                                                     | Place orders                                  |
| Hyperliquid trade ticket       | Relay the exact short-lived intent approved by a connected wallet in accepted deployments | Trade autonomously or reuse an expired intent |
| Polymarket                     | Research, compliance check, preview, prepare external handoff, verify receipt             | Sign or submit an order                       |
| Sui                            | Connect supported wallet, read public state, prepare transfer or publishing preview       | Hold keys or sign on behalf of the user       |
| EVM wallet                     | Detect supported injected wallets and present explicit review flows                       | Ask for a seed phrase or private key          |

### Supported Wallet Language

When verified in the deployed candidate, the website may name:

- MetaMask;
- Coinbase Wallet;
- supported injected EVM wallets;
- Sui Wallet Standard wallets;
- Phantom for supported Sui connection.

Do not claim that a wallet is supported based only on its logo appearing in the UI. Each public wallet must pass connect, account approval, reload, network behavior, rejection, disconnect, and revoked-access acceptance on the release candidate.

### Never Request Or Store

- seed phrases;
- private keys;
- mnemonics;
- wallet exports;
- raw signatures;
- signed payloads;
- exchange secrets;
- API secrets pasted into chat, notes, memory, or MCP tools.

### Financial And Protocol Disclaimer

> Matterhorn Desks provides research, workflow support, and reviewable previews. It does not provide financial advice or guarantee protocol, market, validator, staking, or trading outcomes. Users remain responsible for reviewing and approving every action.

## 12. Privacy And Data

### Approved High-Level Copy

> **Your work stays attached to your workspace.**
>
> Notes, approved memory, outputs, and evidence remain visible and manageable. Matterhorn does not use workspace content for model training by default.

### Desktop Product Truth

The desktop product is local-first. Project files, Notes, Matterhorn Memory, Outputs, receipts, and audit records are stored within the user's local workspace or configured local Matterhorn data boundary.

### Web Product Truth

The public web app is an authenticated companion. It must use a secure, same-origin session and independently authorize each user, organization, and workspace request. It must not expose backend URLs, client bearer tokens, host tokens, or upstream service credentials to the browser.

### Important Qualification

Do not publish “all data always stays on your device.” That statement is only accurate for some desktop-local flows and would be misleading for an authenticated hosted web deployment or external model/provider request.

### Training Policy

Approved copy:

> **No training use by default.**
>
> Workspace content is not used to train models by default. Product feedback may be retained for evaluation, routing, and quality review according to the published privacy policy.

Legal and privacy owners must confirm this language against the production privacy policy and every enabled provider before publication.

## 19. FAQ Copy

### What is Matterhorn Desks?

Matterhorn Desks is an AI workspace made of focused desks for protocol, market, and professional workflows. Each desk combines conversation, context, tools, review, and project evidence.

### How is it different from a chatbot?

A chatbot mainly returns messages. Matterhorn Desks connects a conversation to a project, domain-specific tools, reviewable actions, Notes, user-approved Memory, Outputs, and receipts.

### What is a desk?

A desk is a focused workspace for a particular kind of work. It starts with the relevant context, tools, prompts, safety boundaries, and outputs already in view.

### Which desks are available?

The Public Beta focuses on Bittensor, Hyperliquid, Polymarket, and Longevity. Sui is available as a supported wallet and workflow integration where the deployment and wallet are compatible.

### Can Matterhorn Desks trade automatically?

No. Chat, watches, MCPs, and CLI workflows do not place trades. Hyperliquid supports a separate manual trade ticket only in accepted beta environments, after the user reviews exact terms and signs a short-lived intent in a connected wallet.

### Does Matterhorn Desks place Polymarket orders?

No. The Polymarket desk supports research, compliance checks, previews, watches, and external handoffs. Matterhorn does not sign or submit Polymarket orders.

### Does Matterhorn Desks submit Bittensor transactions?

No. It prepares unsigned actions and external-signer handoffs. The user completes signing and submission with a compatible Bittensor signer outside Matterhorn.

### Does Matterhorn hold my wallet keys?

No. Matterhorn is non-custodial and should never ask for a seed phrase, private key, mnemonic, raw signature, signed payload, wallet export, or exchange secret.

### What does Matterhorn Memory save?

Only records that pass the explicit review flow. Users can confirm, edit, dismiss, search, export, update, or forget saved memory. Chat content is not silently saved as Memory.

### Are Notes and Memory the same?

No. Notes are project-owned working context. A note becomes a Memory suggestion only when the user explicitly chooses that path, and it still requires review.

### What are Outputs?

Outputs are the files and evidence created by work in Matterhorn, including documents, images, previews, receipts, and structured workflow artifacts.

### What are Cautious, Balanced, and Optimistic perspectives?

They change how an answer is framed. They do not change the model's permissions, tools, evidence, wallet review, compliance, or safety requirements.

### Can I use Matterhorn from another AI tool?

Matterhorn MCPs can be configured in supported MCP clients such as Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, and Cursor. Available tools and external dependencies still follow their own permissions and readiness state.

### Is Matterhorn Desks available on desktop and web?

The product is designed desktop-first, with a macOS app and an authenticated web companion planned for the Public Beta. Publish the live links only after the exact release candidate passes acceptance.

### Is Matterhorn Desks financial or medical advice?

No. Protocol and market information is research and workflow support, not financial advice. Longevity workflows are educational and non-medical and do not diagnose, prescribe, or treat conditions.

### Is my data used to train models?

Workspace content is not used for model training by default. The production privacy policy should describe any feedback, evaluation, provider, retention, and hosted-web processing in full.
