# Pendle

## What this skill does
Pendle is a yield tokenization protocol that splits yield-bearing assets into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), enabling fixed yield, leveraged yield exposure, and yield trading. This skill teaches an AI agent how to trade PT/YT, provide liquidity to Pendle pools, and manage yield positions.

## Common operations
### Understanding PT and YT
1. PT (Principal Token): Represents the right to redeem the principal at maturity. Traded at a discount to face value, with the discount representing the fixed yield. At maturity, PT converges 1:1 to the underlying asset.
2. YT (Yield Token): Represents the right to claim all yield generated by the underlying asset until maturity. YT value decays to 0 at maturity. Used for leveraged yield exposure — a small YT position captures yield on a large amount of principal.
3. Every Pendle market has an expiry date. After expiry, PT holders can redeem for the full underlying value, and YT becomes worthless.
4. Check market details via `https://api-v2.pendle.finance/core/api/v1/<chainId>/markets` to get expiry timestamps, implied APY, and total liquidity.

### Swapping PT and YT
1. Query the current market prices via the Pendle API endpoint `/markets/<marketAddress>/swap` or by reading `getPtToAssetRate(marketAddress)` and `getYtToAssetRate(marketAddress)` from the Pendle Router.
2. To buy PT at a discount, encode a `swapExactTokenForPt(routerAddress, marketAddress, exactTokenIn, minPtOut, approxParams)` call. This gives the user a fixed yield position — known APY if held to maturity.
3. To buy YT for leveraged yield, encode `swapExactTokenForYt(routerAddress, marketAddress, exactTokenIn, minYtOut, approxParams)`. Warn the user that YT value erodes to zero at expiry.
4. To sell PT or YT back to the market, use the inverse functions: `swapExactPtForToken` and `swapExactYtForToken`.
5. Submit via `wallet_sendTransaction` targeting the Router contract. Always apply a 0.5%–1% slippage buffer on `minOut` amounts.

### Providing Liquidity
1. Pendle pools hold PT and the underlying asset (SY token). LPs earn swap fees and PENDLE emissions.
2. To add liquidity, the user must provide either (a) PT + SY token in the pool's ratio, or (b) just the underlying SY token (which gets auto-split into PT + SY via the Zap function).
3. Encode `addLiquidityDualSyAndPt(routerAddress, marketAddress, syAmount, ptAmount, minLpOut)` or use `zapIn(syAmount, minLpOut)` for single-sided deposits via the Zap contract.
4. To remove liquidity, encode `removeLiquidityDualSyAndPt(routerAddress, marketAddress, lpAmount, minSyOut, minPtOut)`.
5. Submit via `wallet_sendTransaction`. Remind the user that LP positions are subject to impermanent loss and that PT/SY price relationships shift as the market approaches maturity.

### Maturity and Redemption
1. After a market's maturity timestamp passes, PT holders can redeem PT 1:1 for the underlying asset. Encode `redeemPyToToken(redeemerAddress, pyAddress, netPtIn, minTokenOut)` or call `redeemDueInterestsAndRewards` through the standard interface.
2. YT becomes worthless at maturity. There is no redemption value — YT holders should ensure they sell or exit their YT positions before expiry.
3. Check if a market has matured via `getState(marketAddress)` on the Market contract — look for the `matured` boolean in the state response.
4. After redemption, verify the user receives the underlying asset (e.g., stETH, wstETH, USDC yield tokens). Remind the user that gas is required for the redemption transaction.
5. For vePENDLE holders, redeemed yield can be restaked into new markets for compounding returns.

### Reading Yield Data
1. Query the implied APY of a PT via the formula: `impliedAPY = ((1 / ptRate) ^ (365 / daysToMaturity)) - 1` where `ptRate` is the current PT price as a fraction of 1.
2. Query the underlying yield source rate via `getYieldRate(marketAddress)` to compare implied vs. current yield.
3. Fetch historical yield data from the Pendle subgraph or API to analyze yield trends and project future returns.
4. Present yield metrics clearly: "Buying PT at 0.95 ETH gives you 5.26% fixed APY if held 6 months to maturity" or "Buying YT gives you 20x leveraged exposure to stETH staking yields until maturity."
